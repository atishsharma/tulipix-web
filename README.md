## Tulipix Manager

One Android app for everything on your phone. It works offline, with no account and no tracking.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Home** | Pick up where you left off: now playing, recent, and your storage at a glance |
| **Photos & videos** | Browse by day, Memories from this day, places, and videos that resume |
| **Music** | Songs, albums, artists and playlists, with lyrics, an equaliser and a sleep timer |
| **Books & comics** | EPUB with read-aloud, CBZ/CBR with strip mode and zoom, and PDFs |
| **Search** | One search across songs, videos, photos and documents |
| **Widgets** | Now playing and Memories in five styles and three glass strengths |
| **Private folder** | Encrypted, and opened with your fingerprint, face or screen lock |

## Privacy

Your files never leave your phone. The only optional request is a lyrics look-up, and it is off by default.
Read the [privacy policy](https://tulipix.pro/manager/privacy/) and [terms](https://tulipix.pro/manager/terms/).
