Tulipix Manager (app.tulipix.phone) · Developer: Atish Sharma · Effective 9 November 2026

Privacy policy

In short. Tulipix Manager works on your phone. It has no accounts. We do not collect, store or sell your personal data, and your photos, music, videos, books and documents are never uploaded to us. The Google Play version shows ads through Google AdMob, which receives some device data to do that (described below); one payment removes the ads. The version downloaded from this website has no ads. An optional lyrics look-up is off until you turn it on.

What the app reads on your phone

The app reads files on your phone so it can show and play them. This stays on your phone.

AccessWhy
Photos and videos (and the location stored in a picture, if you allow it)To show your library, the viewer, search, Memories and the town a picture was taken in. The town is worked out on the phone from a list inside the app; no location is sent anywhere.
Music and audioTo list and play your songs, with background playback and lock-screen controls.
Books, comics, PDFs and documentsTo open and read them. You pick them with the system file picker, or open them with the app from another app. (The version downloaded from this website can also look through the phone's storage for them.)

What is sent to the internet

Ads (Google Play version only). The app shows a few ads from Google AdMob, between the shelves on Home and in some Music lists, never in the readers or players. To do this the app asks Google for an ad. That request contains your phone's Advertising ID, your IP address (from which Google can work out an approximate location), and device and app details such as the phone model, the Android version, the app version and language. Google also receives which ads were shown and tapped, and diagnostic information about how the ad code ran. We do not receive any of it, and we send Google none of your files or their names. Google's own policies apply to what it does with this: how Google uses data from partner sites and apps. Where the law asks (for example in the European Union and the United Kingdom), a consent form from Google appears before the first ad, and Settings › Privacy and ad choices reopens it. Remove ads stops all ad requests for good.

Purchase. Remove ads is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the app only that the purchase exists, so the ads can stay off on any phone with the same Google account.

Lyrics. When you turn "Find lyrics online" on (it is off by default; you turn it on in Settings › Playing), the app asks the free lyrics service LRCLIB (lrclib.net) for the words of a song you play. The request contains the song's title, artist, album and length, and, like any web request, your IP address. It contains no account, no device identifier and none of your files. The words that come back are kept on your phone. Leave it off, or turn it off again, and nothing is sent. LRCLIB's own policy applies to what it does with requests.

The direct version from this website makes no requests to the internet except the optional lyrics look-up: it has no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. The Google Play version makes no other requests than those above and has no analytics or crash-reporting code of its own.

The Private folder

Files you move into Private are encrypted on your phone with a key kept in Android's secure storage. We cannot open them and cannot recover them: if you uninstall the app or lose the key, they are gone.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and do not hand it to anyone ourselves. The only data that leaves the app does so as described above: ad requests go to Google AdMob and optional lyrics requests go to LRCLIB. Google Play and Android handle installation and updates under Google's own policies. If you download the APK from this website instead, the website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visits (such as your IP address) under GitHub's own policy; this website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts, fonts or trackers.

Keeping and deleting data

Everything the app keeps (its settings, saved lyrics, whether ads are removed, the Private folder) is on your phone. Clear it in Android's Settings › Apps › Tulipix Manager › Storage, or uninstall the app. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing for us to delete. Data Google holds from ads is managed in your Google Account (My Ad Center) and in Android's Settings › Privacy › Ads.

Children

The app is not aimed at children under 13, and ads are not meant for them. It does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date.
